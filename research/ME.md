# ME.md

## Master's Preferences

- Primary goal: pursue a strong **Master's as preparation for a future PhD/research career**.
- Prefer **research-oriented, academically rigorous** programmes over purely industry-focused degrees.
- Core interests:
  - **AI / Machine Learning**
  - **Computer Science**
  - **Data Science**
  - **Statistics**
  - **Cybersecurity**
  - **Computational Science & Engineering (CSE)**
- Strong preference for programmes with:
  - strong theoretical foundations;
  - substantial mathematical/statistical depth where relevant;
  - active research groups and faculty aligned with my interests;
  - meaningful Master's thesis/research opportunities;
  - a strong environment for progressing to a PhD.
- I care more about **curriculum, research strength, faculty fit, and PhD potential** than the programme title or rankings alone.
- Prefer **English-taught programmes**, primarily in Europe.
- Two-year / ~120 ECTS Master's programmes are particularly attractive because they provide more room for advanced coursework and research.
- Cost, scholarships, and admission feasibility matter, but **academic and research fit are the primary considerations**.
- I want a balanced shortlist of ambitious, target, and safer universities, while keeping the overall academic quality high.
