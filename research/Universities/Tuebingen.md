## 00. Overview

### Rankings

- One of the world's leading centres for Machine Learning research
- Home to the **Cluster of Excellence "Machine Learning: New Perspectives for Science"**

- ELLIS Unit Tübingen — member of the European Laboratory for Learning and Intelligent Systems

- **€1,500/semester** (Baden-Württemberg regulation for non-EU students)

## 05. Research

### The Tübingen ML Ecosystem

Tübingen is arguably the single strongest location for ML research in continental Europe:

- **Cluster of Excellence "Machine Learning: New Perspectives for Science"**
- **Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems** — Empirical Inference, Autonomous Motion, Haptic Intelligence
- **Cyber Valley** — Europe's largest AI consortium (Max Planck + Universities + Industry)
- **ELLIS Unit Tübingen** — European Laboratory for Learning and Intelligent Systems
- **Amazon Research Tübingen**
- **Bosch Center for AI (nearby Stuttgart)**

### Key Research Areas

- Deep Learning Theory
- Probabilistic Machine Learning
- Causal Inference & Discovery
- Computer Vision
- Reinforcement Learning
- Representation Learning
- Robotics & Autonomous Systems
- Generative Models
- ML for Science (Physics, Biology, Climate)

## 09. Notes

- Tübingen's ML M.Sc. is one of the most research-oriented ML programmes in Europe.
- The ML ecosystem (Cluster of Excellence + Max Planck + Cyber Valley + ELLIS) is unmatched in Germany.
- The city is small and charming but not a metropolis. Ideal for focused research.
- Baden-Württemberg tuition (€1,500/semester) applies — same as KIT.
- Application deadline (~April) is much later than most other universities on this list.
- Ideal for students specifically targeting ML/AI research careers and PhD pathways.
- Amazon Research and Bosch Center for AI provide industry thesis/internship opportunities.
- The Informatik M.Sc. is primarily German-taught; ML M.Sc. is the English-taught option.
