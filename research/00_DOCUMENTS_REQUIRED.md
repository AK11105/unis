# Documents Required — Unified View

> **Applicant:** Indian national, CS background
> **Target:** Autumn/Fall 2027 intake
> **Last updated:** 15 August 2026

---

## MUST — Required by ALL universities

These documents are needed for every single application. Prepare once, reuse everywhere.

| # | Document | Notes |
|---|----------|-------|
| 1 | **Passport** | Valid through at least 2028. Colour scan. |
| 2 | **Bachelor's Degree Certificate** | Or provisional/expected completion certificate if not yet graduated. |
| 3 | **Official Transcript of Records** | Sealed/certified. All semesters. |
| 4 | **English Language Certificate** | IELTS Academic or TOEFL iBT. See per-university minimums below. |
| 5 | **Curriculum Vitae (CV)** | Academic CV format. |
| 6 | **Statement of Purpose / Motivation Letter** | Tailor per university but structure can be reused. |

## Preparation Priority

### Start NOW (long lead time)
1. **APS Certificate** — 4–8 weeks processing
2. **GRE** — study + test + score delivery
3. **IELTS/TOEFL** — study + test + score delivery
4. **Recommendation letters** — approach professors early

### Prepare by October 2026
5. Transcripts (sealed copies)
6. CV (academic format)
7. Module descriptions document
8. Grading system explanation

### Prepare by November 2026 (earliest deadlines)
9. Statement of Purpose (ETH version first — Nov 30 deadline)
10. ETH-specific: High school cert, Declaration of Consent, ESOP pre-proposal
11. All recommendation letter writers notified and submitting
