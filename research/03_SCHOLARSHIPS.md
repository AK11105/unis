## Cross-University Scholarships (Germany)

### Deutschlandstipendium
| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Amount** | €300/month (€3,600/year) for 12 months, renewable |

### DAAD Scholarships (multiple programmes)
| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|

### HiWi (Research Assistant) Positions — All German Universities
| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Pay** | €10–16/hr (typical €12–14/hr). Up to 20 hrs/week (usually 10–19 hrs). Monthly gross: ~€400–1,000+ depending on hours. |
| **Covers** | Living costs supplement |
| **Eligibility** | Enrolled students. Positions at university chairs, MPI, DFKI, CISPA, Fraunhofer, etc. |
| **Competitiveness** | Moderate to Low — many positions available, especially in CS/AI. Easier to get after first semester. |
| **Application** | Direct to professors/labs. No central application. Networking critical. |
| **Note** | Not a scholarship but the most *reliable* source of funding for German Master's students. |

## 4. KIT (Karlsruhe Institute of Technology)

**Tuition**: **€1,500/semester** (Baden-Württemberg international student fee) + semester fee ~€190/semester.

| Scholarship | Amount | Covers | Competitiveness | Application | Source |
|-------------|--------|--------|-----------------|-------------|--------|
| **Tuition Fee Exemption (§6 LHGebG)** | €1,500/semester saved | Tuition | Moderate — limited slots for "highly gifted" students | Apply before enrollment/re-registration | https://www.intl.kit.edu |
| **Deutschlandstipendium** | €300/month | Living (partial) | Moderate | After enrollment | kit.edu |
| **HiWi positions** | €400–1,000/month (median €12/hr at KIT) | Living | Low-Moderate | Direct to chairs | N/A |
| **DAAD** | €992/month + extras | Tuition + Living | High | Via DAAD portal | daad.de |
| **Heinrich Böll / KAS** | €934–992/month | Living + can cover tuition | High | Separate | See above |

**Tuition Fee Waiver Details (§6 LHGebG)**:
- Exemption criteria include: cooperation agreements, "highly gifted" students (top academic performance, 80%+ ECTS in allotted time), refugees, disability, bilateral agreements
- Priority given to nationals of ACP countries (under Cotonou/Partnership Agreement) and least-developed countries
- India is NOT in the priority category, but highly gifted Indian students CAN qualify if slots remain
- Application: submit proof of outstanding academic results before enrollment

**Realistic funding strategy**: Tuition (€1,500/sem) is the main extra cost. Push hard for fee waiver application. HiWi + Deutschlandstipendium can cover living. DAAD/Foundation scholarships cover tuition automatically if awarded.

## Key Recommendations for an Indian CS/AI Student

1. **Always apply to Deutschlandstipendium** at whichever German uni you attend — it's €300/month for essentially filling out a form.

2. **DAAD is the gold standard** for Germany — apply early and to programmes that specifically list DAAD EPOS funding.

3. **HiWi is the most reliable funding** in Germany — almost every strong CS/AI student gets one by semester 2. Budget for this.

4. **For France (IP Paris / Saclay)**: Get your institution to nominate you for Eiffel. Apply to Charpak (India-specific!). IP Paris PhD Track is the highest-value option if PhD is planned.

5. **For Switzerland (ETH/EPFL)**: Apply in Round 1 (Dec 15) and tick the scholarship box. These are purely merit-based — your academic record IS the application.

6. **For Sweden (KTH)**: KTH India Scholarship is your primary strategy. Without it, KTH is not financially viable.

7. **Foundation scholarships (Böll, KAS, FES)**: Heinrich Böll is the most accessible for Indian students (no German requirement). KAS/FES require B2+ German — only realistic if you already speak German well.

8. **Baden-Württemberg (KIT/Tübingen)**: Always apply for tuition fee exemption (§6). Even if denied, the €1,500/semester is manageable with a HiWi job.
