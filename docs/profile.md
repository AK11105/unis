## Programme Preferences

- **Research-oriented and academically rigorous** — not purely industry-focused
- **English-taught**, primarily in Europe
- **2-year / 120 ECTS** preferred — more room for advanced coursework and research
- Strong mathematical/statistical depth
- Active research groups aligned with my interests
- Meaningful Master's thesis opportunities
- Strong environment for progressing to a PhD

---

## Selection Philosophy

I care more about **curriculum, research strength, faculty fit, and PhD potential** than programme title or rankings alone. The shortlist balances ambitious, target, and safer universities while keeping overall academic quality high.
