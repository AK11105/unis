# [Institut Polytechnique de Paris (IP Paris)](https://www.ip-paris.fr/en)

> **Country:** France | **Tier:** Moderate

=== "Crux"

    | | |
    |---|---|
    | **Cost** | ~€508 total · €900–1,300/mo |
    | **Deadline** | ~Late autumn – March |
    | **GRE** | Optional |
    | **English** | English cert if required |
    | **Duration** | 4 semesters · 120 ECTS · English-taught |

    ---

    **Why it's here:** €254/year buys access to 6 elite French schools (Polytechnique, Télécom Paris, ENSTA, ENSAE, ENPC, Télécom SudParis). The PhD Track offers guaranteed doctoral funding — a rare and powerful differentiator. Exceptional mathematics + AI training at minimal cost, with a clear research career path built in.

    **Programmes:** AI · Computer Science · Math for AI

    **Watch out:** Information fragmented across constituent schools. PhD Track is the key differentiator — apply for it specifically. Application timeline varies by programme.

=== "Detail"

    ## Overview

    ### Institution Profile

    - **Country:** France
    - **Language:** English
    - **Duration:** 2 Years (120 ECTS)
    - **Intake:** September
    - **Degree:** Master of Science

    ### What Makes IP Paris Attractive

    - One of Europe's strongest engineering and mathematics institutions
    - IP Paris comprises **6 founding schools:**
        - École Polytechnique
        - Télécom Paris
        - ENSTA Paris
        - ENSAE Paris
        - École des Ponts ParisTech (ENPC)
        - Télécom SudParis
    - Excellent reputation in AI, Machine Learning, Mathematics, Optimization, and Computer Science
    - Strong industry collaborations
    - Excellent pathway into PhD and research careers

    ### Tuition

    - Approximately **€254/year** (French national Master's fees, subject to annual revision)
    - IP Paris follows the French public university system for most national Master's programmes
    - Some specialised programmes may have different tuition

    ### Scholarships

    - **IP Paris Scholarships** (available depending on programme)
    - **PhD Track Scholarships:** Master's excellence scholarship + Doctoral funding package
    - **Campus France Scholarships**
    - **Eiffel Excellence Scholarship** (available for eligible international applicants)

    ## Admission

    ### Timeline

    Applications generally open in **late autumn** and close between **January–March** depending on the programme. Monitor the programme page for exact deadlines.

    ### Application Platform

    - **[IP Paris Application Portal](https://www.ip-paris.fr/en/education/masters/how-to-apply)**


    ### Academic Requirements

    - Bachelor's degree equivalent to 180 ECTS
    - Strong background in:
        - Mathematics
        - Computer Science
        - Programming
        - Probability
        - Linear Algebra
        - Statistics
        - Algorithms

    ### English

    - Proof of English proficiency if required by the programme

    ### Evaluation Criteria

    - Academic performance & GPA
    - Mathematical preparation
    - Computer Science preparation
    - Quality of previous university
    - Motivation & Research potential
    - Relevant work/research experience
    - Publications (if any)
    - Recommendation letters

    ### Required Documents

    - Passport
    - Bachelor's Degree
    - Official Transcripts
    - CV
    - Motivation Letter
    - English Proficiency Certificate
    - Recommendation Letters (if requested)
    - GRE/GMAT (only if programme requests or optional)
    - Additional programme-specific forms

    ## Notes & Sources

    === "Notes"

        - Tuition ~€254/year (French national rate) — same as Paris-Saclay, incredibly affordable.
        - IP Paris includes École Polytechnique, Télécom Paris, ENSTA Paris, ENSAE Paris — all elite French schools.
        - PhD Track is a unique option: guaranteed doctoral funding after Master's.
        - Research strength in optimization, mathematics for AI, probabilistic modelling.
        - Internship (4-6 months) is mandatory — good industry exposure.
        - Application typically late autumn to January-March depending on programme.
        - Key advantage: world-class mathematics + AI at minimal cost. Access to PhD Track funding.
        - Key risk: information somewhat fragmented across constituent schools.
