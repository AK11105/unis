# [University of Bonn](https://www.uni-bonn.de/en)

> **Country:** Germany | **Tier:** Target


---

## Overview

### Rankings

- University of Excellence (Exzellenzuniversität)
- Strong Computer Science department with excellent research output
- Top-tier in Mathematics (Fields Medal connection) and AI

### Tuition

- **No tuition fees** (NRW — North Rhine-Westphalia state, same as RWTH)
- **Semester contribution:** ~€345–363/semester (includes Deutschlandsemesterticket for nationwide transport; WS 2026/27: €363.07)

### Estimated Living Cost

- €800–1,100/month (Bonn is moderately priced)

### Scholarships

- DAAD
- Deutschlandstipendium (€300/month)
- Research assistantships (HiWi)
- Bonn International Graduate School (BIGS) — PhD funding

### Research Strengths

- Computer Vision (one of the best in Germany)
- Machine Learning
- Robotics
- Autonomous Intelligent Systems
- High Performance Computing
- Mathematics (world-class)
- Algorithms

### Industry & Research Connections

- Fraunhofer IAIS (Intelligent Analysis and Information Systems)
- Fraunhofer SCAI (Algorithms and Scientific Computing)
- Hausdorff Center for Mathematics (Cluster of Excellence)
- Lamarr Institute for Machine Learning and AI
- Deutsche Telekom

## Target Programs

### Computer Science (M.Sc.)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Duration | 4 semesters |
| Credits | 120 ECTS |
| Language | **English** |
| Start | Winter Semester |
| Thesis | Mandatory |

#### Highlights

- **Fully English-taught** (German NOT required)
- Strong mathematical foundation
- Research-oriented
- Computer Vision and Robotics strengths
- Access to Lamarr Institute, Fraunhofer IAIS
- Consecutive programme (builds on CS bachelor)
- **Extremely competitive for Indian applicants** — >99% rejection rate for applicants from Bangladesh, India, Nepal, Pakistan, Afghanistan

#### Admission

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent
- Sound knowledge of compulsory CS bachelor subjects
- **Scientific bachelor's thesis required** (minimum 12 ECTS, 750+ lines of text)
- Strong theoretical CS background (Algorithms & Complexity mandatory)
- English C1 required: IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 95
- MOI letter NOT accepted for non-European applicants
- APS certificate required for Indian applicants
- Application via uni-bonn.de portal

---

## Admission Requirements

### Academic Requirements

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or Cyber Security (or equivalent)
- Strong background in core CS subjects (algorithms, programming, databases, OS, networking)
- Bachelor must be completed before start of semester (hard deadline)

### English Language

- **C1 required:** IELTS Academic 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 95 (new scoring: 5.0)
- MOI letter NOT accepted for non-European applicants

### German Language

- **Not required** for M.Sc. Computer Science (programme is fully English-taught)

### GRE

- Not required

### APS Certificate

- Required for Indian applicants
