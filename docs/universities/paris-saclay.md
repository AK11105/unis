## Overview

### Rankings

- One of Europe's strongest research universities
- Globally recognized for Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics and Data Science
- Strong connections with CNRS, INRIA, CEA and top French research laboratories

- **≈ €254/year** (subject to yearly revision)
- Université Paris-Saclay fully exempts most international students from differentiated tuition fees, allowing non-EU students to pay the standard national fee

## Target Programme

### M.Sc. Computer Science (AI Track / Parcours Intelligence Artificielle)

The Artificial Intelligence Master's track (within the M.Sc. Computer Science programme) provides advanced education in modern AI with a strong emphasis on research, practical implementation, and responsible AI. The diploma awarded is a **Master's degree in Computer Science**.

#### Core Topics

- Machine Learning
- Statistical Learning
- Deep Learning
- Natural Language Processing
- Reinforcement Learning
- Generative AI
- Frugal AI
- Scientific Machine Learning
- Optimization
- Responsible AI
- Fairness & Bias
- AI Ethics

#### Skills Developed

- Build production-ready ML and Deep Learning systems
- Develop Generative AI applications
- Train Reinforcement Learning models
- Apply NLP techniques
- Analyze and visualize complex datasets
- Design ethical and trustworthy AI systems
- Build AI solutions for healthcare, finance, robotics and industry

#### Programme Objectives

- Strong mathematical foundations
- Statistical modelling
- Optimization methods
- Practical AI implementation (TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn)
- Responsible AI & Explainability
- AI Safety
- Research preparation & Industry readiness

#### Programme Capacity

- **25 students** — admission is highly competitive

## Admission

### Academic Background

Preferred degrees:

- Computer Science
- Mathematics
- Applied Mathematics
- Physics
- AI-related Engineering

### Expected Background

- Probability & Statistics
- Linear Algebra & Calculus
- Scientific Programming
- Data Visualization

### Application Period (2027 Intake)

Expected (based on previous cycle):

- Opens: **January**
- Closes: **March**
- Programme starts: Late August

### Required Documents

**Mandatory:**

- Passport
- Curriculum Vitae
- Motivation Letter
- Official Transcripts
- List of previous university studies
- Course Selection Sheet
- English Proficiency (minimum B2 or proof of English-medium instruction)
- Recommendation Letter or Internship Evaluation
- Completed Programme Questionnaire
- Self-Evaluation Form
- List of other Paris-Saclay programme preferences

**Optional:**

- GRE / GMAT
- Publications
- Extra certificates

### Application Process

1. Apply through the INCEPTION platform
2. Upload all required documents
3. Programme evaluation
4. Admission decision
5. If selected for the International Master's Scholarship, complete the scholarship application
6. Apply for visa and enrolment

!!! info "Indian applicants"
    Applicants from countries using the **Études en France (EEF)** procedure complete the Campus France/EEF process after receiving an admission letter.

## Notes

- Tuition is extremely low (~€254/year) — best value of all target universities for non-EU students.
- Only 25 students per cohort for the AI programme — highly selective despite being "Moderate" tier.
- The Paris-Saclay International Master's Scholarship (€10,000/year + €900 travel) targets future PhD candidates.
- Application platform is INCEPTION (not Campus France for the admission itself, though EEF process follows).
- Indian applicants go through Études en France (EEF) procedure after receiving admission letter.
- CNRS, INRIA, CEA, LISN collaboration = world-class research infrastructure.
- Living costs in Paris region are moderate (€900-1,300/month), much cheaper than Switzerland.
- English B2 minimum — relatively lenient language requirement.
- Applications expected Jan-March based on previous cycle (15 Jan - 16 March).
- Key advantage: exceptional research at almost zero cost. Key risk: small cohort, high selectivity.
