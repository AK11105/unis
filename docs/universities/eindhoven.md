# Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e)

> **Country:** Netherlands | **Tier:** Target

---

## Overview

### Rankings

- Strong technical university in the Netherlands
- Particularly known for Data Science, AI, Process Mining (invented here), and Engineering
- Close ties to Brainport Eindhoven — one of Europe's leading tech/innovation ecosystems

### Tuition

- **Non-EU (2026-2027):** €21,700/year
- **Non-EU (2027-2028):** €22,400/year
- **EU/EFTA:** ~€2,694/year
- **Total estimated cost (2 years, Non-EU):** ~€43,400–44,100

### Programme Duration

- All Master's programmes: **2 years (120 ECTS)**

### Estimated Living Cost

- €900–1,300/month (Eindhoven is cheaper than Amsterdam/Delft)

### Scholarships

- TU/e Scholarship (tuition waiver or partial)
- Holland Scholarship (€5,000 one-time)
- Amandus H. Lundqvist Scholarship
- ASML Technology Scholarship

### Research Strengths

- Process Mining (world-leading — invented at TU/e)
- Machine Learning
- Distributed Systems
- Embedded Systems
- Computer Graphics
- Cybersecurity
- Software Engineering
- Smart Mobility

### Industry Connections

- **ASML** (world's largest semiconductor equipment maker — HQ in Eindhoven region)
- Philips
- NXP Semiconductors
- DAF Trucks
- VDL Group
- Brainport Eindhoven ecosystem (High Tech Campus)
- Booking.com (nearby)

## Notes

- TU/e is expensive for non-EU (~€21,700/year, ~€44K total for 2 years — similar to TU Delft).
- ASML connection is unique — one of the world's most valuable companies, HQ nearby.
- Process Mining is TU/e's flagship contribution to Data Science — if interested, nowhere better.
- Brainport Eindhoven is Europe's #1 tech region by patent density — excellent industry access.
- Eindhoven is more affordable and less crowded than Amsterdam/Delft.
- EAISI (AI Systems Institute) provides structured AI research collaboration.
- Less globally famous than TU Delft but strong in its specialties.
